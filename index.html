Great Cut Dike
05 · Out to the fields

Most of the Dolores leaves through the side door, into the San Juan basin.

The Dolores Project serves about 61,660 irrigated acres: full service around Dove Creek and Towaoc, supplemental water for the Montezuma Valley. A full supply is 22 inches per acre. 2026 was one of the worst seasons on record.
